We are thrilled to announce **CESS Network Course Season II** in cooperation with **University of Brasilia (UnB)**, an immersive learning experience designed to equip participants with cutting-edge decentralized data infrastructure knowledge and hands-on development skills. This cohort includes both learning & building opportunities with exciting rewards!

This course is an in-depth journey into the future of decentralized data value infrastructure. This comprehensive 10-episode series is designed to provide you with a strong understanding of the CESS Network, from its foundational principles to hands-on development. You will learn how CESS is revolutionizing data management and delivery through its decentralized solutions.

You'll also gain practical experience with CESS APIs, developing dApps, setting up and running nodes, and even adding custom features to the CESS source code.

## 📖 What You’ll Learn

* **Blockchain & Web3 Fundamentals**: Before diving into the CESS Network, we'll first explore the fundamentals of Blockchain and Web3 to build a solid foundation.
* **Introduction to CESS**: What it is, its development history, and why it's a game changer in the decentralized storage space.
* **CESS Architecture and Key Technologies**: Dive deep into the network's structure and technologies.
* **The CESS Ecosystem**: Explore user roles, participation benefits, and current applications.
* **Node Setup and CESS Accounts**: Learn about becoming a node, and setting up CESS accounts.
* **Consensus and Storage Node Demos**: A step-by-step guide to running consensus and storage nodes.
* **DeOSS and REST API**: Discover decentralized object storage on CESS, and how to use the REST API for scalable and secure data storage.
* **dApp Development**: Build dApps using Ink! and Solidity smart contracts on CESS, complete with practical examples.
* **Custom Pallets**: Learn how to build and integrate custom pallets to contribute to the network’s development.

This course equips you with the knowledge and tools to engage with the CESS Network, whether you're a developer looking to integrate CESS services or a user exploring the benefits of decentralized data storage. You'll also have access to developer bounties, grants, and opportunities for project incubation.

Join us to unlock the potential of decentralized data management with CESS!

## Cohort Participation & Rewards

#### 1️⃣ Course Completion NFT (Exercises Requirement)

**How to Earn the NFT**

* Score at least 60% of the Exercises to qualify for the CESS Course Completion NFT. (The exercise score will contribute 20% to the final course score.)
* The NFT serves as proof of participation and completion, valuable for future job opportunities and Web3 credentials.

**Exercises Format**

* Multiple-choice questions covering CESS Course.
* Ensures students gain strong theoretical knowledge before competing in the project phase.

#### 2️⃣ Project Submission & Prizes

**Eligibility**

* Students should submit and present a project orally. (The exercise score will contribute 80% to the final course score.)
* Projects can be submitted individually or in teams (max 2 members).
* Must utilize CESS in one of the following ways:
  * CESS Storage – DeOSS, CDN, or decentralized storage
  * CESS Smart Contracts – Ink! or Solidity on CESS
  * CESS Pallets – Custom Substrate runtime modules

Final Scoring System (for Teams & Individuals) Each team member’s final score is calculated as:

> _Final Score = (Exercise Score \* 0.2) + (Project Score \* 0.8)_

To determine the team’s final ranking, we use the simple average of all individual final scores within a team:

> _Team Final Score = Sum of Individual Final Score / Number of Team Members_

**Example Calculation for a 2-Member Team**

| Student | Exercise Score (20%) | Project Score (80%) | Final Score |
| ------- | -------------------- | ------------------- | ----------- |
| Alice   | 90%                  | 85%                 | 86%         |
| Bob     | 75%                  | 85%                 | 83%         |

Team Final Score = (86 + 83) / 2 = 84.5

**Evaluation Criteria for Projects (80%)**

* **Technical Innovation** – How effectively CESS is integrated.
* **Functionality & Usability** – How well the project works.
* **Presentation & Documentation** – Clarity, explanation & code quality.

**Submission Format**

* **GitHub Repository** with project source code.
* **README** file explaining:
  * Project overview & CESS integration.
  * Setup instructions for testing the project.
  * Short demo video (3-5 min) presenting the project.
